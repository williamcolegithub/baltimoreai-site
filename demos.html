Demo 01

RAG over a fictional fantasy wiki

A made-up lore wiki ("The Reach of Halmar"). 240 questions in a golden set. We try four versions of the retrieval + prompt. The verify gate blocks the fifth.

Demo 02

Story critique bot

A bot that grades short fiction on a rubric. Before: free-form responses, no scoring. After: a Pydantic-typed rubric, judge model, and a golden set of 120 stories with known scores.
