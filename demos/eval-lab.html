Does it know when it doesn't know?

Same question, same model, same documents. Move the clock forward and watch it lose confidence, then refuse to guess once the records go stale.
Probe question What is the city's vacant-building demolition budget?
What just happened: the corpus tops out at the FY2023 council minutes — $9.4M was enacted, while $14M for FY2024 was only proposed (vote scheduled Oct 2023, outcome not in the docs). So as the as-of date passes that record, the model stops asserting a figure, drops its confidence, and abstains rather than hallucinating a current budget. That's calibration and temporal awareness — not just accuracy.
