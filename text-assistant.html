What it is

You text a question or a task. The assistant texts back. There is no app to install.

You can use it to:

  • Set reminders and get them by text.
  • Keep lists and notes and add to them by text.
  • Plan a day, a trip, or a project.
  • Ask questions and get a short written answer.

Messages come from +1 (844) 925-2512. Save that number so you know it is us.

Replies are written by an AI model. They can be wrong. It is not medical, legal, or financial advice.
